5. Pitch and dP/dt

Dive {{ p.dives|join(", ") }}: pitch against the rate of pressure change, colored by PHASE. Descent sits at negative pitch and positive dP/dt, ascent at positive pitch and negative dP/dt; inflection lies between them; transition sits at steep negative pitch with dP/dt near zero (the start of the dive) and at positive pitch with dP/dt still positive (the bottom). Pitch is interpolated in time onto the pressure rows.
