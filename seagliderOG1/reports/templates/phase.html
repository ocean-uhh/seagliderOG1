3. Pitch and dP/dt

Dive {{ p.dives|join(", ") }}: pitch against the rate of pressure change, colored by PHASE. Descent sits at negative pitch and positive dP/dt, ascent at positive pitch and negative dP/dt; inflection lies between them. Descent points at steep negative pitch and dP/dt near zero are the glider bleeding at the surface before the flare; ascent points at positive pitch with dP/dt still positive are the glider still sinking after the apogee ends. Pitch is interpolated in time onto the pressure data points.

{{ 5 if p.scatter_png else 4 }}. Seaglider state table

Every state the Seaglider basestation numbers in gc_state_state, in begin/end pairs. Each begin state of a pair sets the PHASE the OG1 phase translation table specifies the transition, held until the next listed pair begins; a pair the table does not list is ignored, so the phase before it holds. Count: how many times the begin state appears in this file. Ended with: the end-of-phase codes (gc_state_eop_code) the end state carried. Names and numbers: the basestation's own list.
