5. Pitch and dP/dt

Dive {{ p.dives|join(", ") }}: pitch against the rate of pressure change, colored by PHASE. Descent sits at negative pitch and positive dP/dt, ascent at positive pitch and negative dP/dt; inflection lies between them. Descent rows at steep negative pitch and dP/dt near zero are the glider bleeding at the surface before the flare; ascent rows at positive pitch with dP/dt still positive are the glider still sinking after the apogee ends. Pitch is interpolated in time onto the pressure rows.
